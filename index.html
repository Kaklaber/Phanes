PhanesLocal image tools

Turn an image into something else.

Three small tools, one file. Everything runs in your browser -- nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Tessellate Image → HTML/CSS

Rebuilds an image as a mosaic of styled HTML/CSS tiles.

Thoth Image → ASCII

Converts photos and live camera frames into ASCII art.

Trace Image → SVG

Traces a bitmap into clean, scalable SVG paths.

Runs entirely in your browser. No uploads, no accounts. MIT licensed.
